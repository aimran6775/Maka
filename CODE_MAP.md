Left after phase 4 (every other item of `docs/PHASE4_BRIEF.md` Parts A to E is in this build):

- Google Trends (no official API; D86). The trend sources are Wikipedia pageviews, Hacker News, Reddit's public listings and Etsy's Open API.
- A connector that reports outgoing charges. Purchase reconciliation (step 17, D148) asks every connector for `outgoingCharges` and none has it: Stripe, Shopify, Etsy and the ad platforms report money in and ad spend, not the card the supplier charged. Every purchase therefore ends `no_source` until a bank or card connector exists; the row says so.
- An import beyond text (step 19, D150). Sessions, databases, OAuth state and tool configurations of an OpenClaw or Hermes home are never read, by design; only the persona, the instructions, the memory text and the model name come over. A richer import would be a new decision, not a gap in this one.
